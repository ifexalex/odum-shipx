Why Shipx

Stop running your business on WhatsApp and spreadsheets.

Most China–Nigeria forwarders juggle a dozen disconnected tools. Shipx replaces all of it with one system your whole team — and your customers — actually log into.

The old way

  • ✕ Packing lists in Excel, emailed back and forth, versions everywhere
  • ✕ Billing calculated by hand — FX and CBM errors cost you money
  • ✕ Customers call and message constantly: "where is my cargo?"
  • ✕ No record of who changed what, when a dispute arises
  • ✕ China office and Lagos office working off different numbers

The Shipx way

  • ✓ Packing lists created once, approved in-app, locked to a container
  • ✓ Billing auto-computed from live CBM × FX rates, every time
  • ✓ Customers track their own cargo and pay online — fewer calls
  • ✓ Every action audit-logged with before/after history
  • ✓ One source of truth shared across both offices, in real time
Everything in one place

The full operation, end to end.

From the moment cargo enters your Guangzhou warehouse to the customer's pickup in Lagos.

Containers & tracking

Create containers, track them through 8 stages from China warehouse to Lagos pickup, with a clear China-side / Nigeria-side handoff.

Packing lists

Carton-level capture with auto-CBM, an approval workflow, and bulk import from Excel when you're processing a whole container at once.

Billing on autopilot

Every bill computed from your configured CBM and USD→NGN rates. Misc charges, carryovers, storage fees and CBM disputes all handled.

Claims & customs

Log damage, missing items and seized goods. Verify manifests on arrival, manage customs clearance, and resolve disputes with a full paper trail.

Customer self-service

Your customers register, track shipments, see their bill, pay with Paystack, and file claims — without tying up your staff on the phone.

Release notes & pickup

Generate release notes the moment a bill is settled, with QR codes your warehouse scans to confirm pickup — partial releases included.
