01

Brand

How Shipx sits inside the ODUM family — and where it earns its own voice.

Positioning

The operational arm of ODUM.

If ODUM is "the lion of the business world" — bold, decisive, culturally rooted — Shipx is that posture turned into throughput. Engineered. Precise. Always in motion. The brand carries ODUM's full weight while specializing for the logistics surface: dense data, time pressure, multi-role collaboration across continents.

Sub-brand relationship

Shared spine. Specialized accent.

  • Inherits from ODUM: the kola-nut icon, Odum Green family, Averta/Kumbh type system, beveled shape vocabulary, voice of confident leadership.
  • Specialized for Shipx: Kola Copper CTA accent, motion-forward interactions, shipping-specific patterns (tracking, address blocks, API surfaces), role accents for multi-tenant portals.
Audience 01

Logistics operators

Internal staff in Lagos & Guangzhou clearing shipments, generating release notes, resolving discrepancies. Live in dense tables. Need scanability above all.

Audience 02

Customers shipping cargo

SMBs and individuals tracking a container or carton. Reach the product mostly on mobile. Need confidence: where is it, when does it arrive, what do I owe.

Audience 03

Developers integrating

Partners hitting our API to embed Shipx in their checkout, marketplace, or ERP. Need rigor: predictable shapes, clear errors, code samples that paste cleanly.

02

Logo & Icon

The ODUM kola-nut is carried over verbatim. The Shipx wordmark anchors the lockup.

The ODUM mark

Three-lobed kola nut (Oji Ikenga)

Body · Mind · Spirit. Rooted in Igbo symbolism, the three lobes represent the complete being — strength, balance, responsibility. Beveled rounded corners soften geometric structure without surrendering it.

In Shipx, the mark stands unchanged. Use it at scales from 16px (favicon) to print without modification.

The Shipx wordmark

"shipx" — lowercase, Manrope ExtraBold

Lowercase for approachability; ExtraBold for the lion's confidence. The x takes the Kola Copper accent — it's both the brand's punctuation and the visual ligature that connects "ship" to ODUM's earth-toned heritage.

Clear space

Minimum clear space equals the width of one lobe (x) on all sides of the mark or lockup. Never crowd the kola-nut against other elements.

DoUse the kola-nut on green, white, near-black, or Kola Copper.
Don'tRecolor the lobes individually or break the trefoil rotation.
DoPair the mark with the Shipx wordmark in horizontal lockups.
Don'tRender the mark on busy photo backgrounds without a solid scrim.
DoMaintain at least 16px height for legibility (favicon use only).
Don'tStretch, skew, rotate, or apply effects (drop shadow, glow, bevel).

04

Typography

Averta for display. Kumbh Sans for body. JetBrains Mono for codes & tracking numbers.

Display · Averta (Kostas Bartsokas)

Aa Bb Cc

Averta carries the Odum identity — its geometric construction and open counters in d, u, m echo the wordmark. Use it sparingly but boldly; headlines should land.

Licensing: Commercial via Type Department. Fallback: Manrope (free, geometric) — used here.

Body · Kumbh Sans (Saurabh Sharma)

Aa Bb Cc

The workhorse. Stays legible at small sizes — essential for dense logistics tables and form-heavy screens. Free via Google Fonts; complete weight range.

Use for: all body copy, labels, table cells, helper text, button text.

Mono · JetBrains Mono

Voice & Tone

Active, ownership-driven, confident. Heritage of the lion — engineered for the operator.

We sound like
  • Active, not passive: "Lead every shipment", not "Shipments can be tracked".
  • Specific, not generic: "Container SHX-04792 cleared customs", not "Status updated".
  • Calmly authoritative — the lion doesn't shout.
  • Plain English over jargon, but never dumbed-down for operators.
We don't sound like
  • Over-friendly: no exclamation marks, no "Oops!", no emoji.
  • Corporate-vague: no "leverage", "synergy", "best-in-class".
  • Apologetic: errors are direct and tell the user what to do next.
  • Trend-chasing: no slang, no "fire", no "vibes".

16

Motion

Motion implies cargo in motion — direct, decisive, never decorative.

Ease — primary
cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1) · 240ms

Default for panels, drawers, modal entry. Confident in, settled at rest.

Spring — feedback
cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1) · 420ms

Reserved for arrival moments — toasts landing, stat numbers ticking up.

Pulse — live
ease-in-out · 1.6s loop

Currently active step on the tracking timeline. The only loop in the system.

Durations

120ms — micro (hover, ripple)
240ms — UI (panel, modal)
420ms — feedback (toast spring)
1600ms — live indicators (loop)

When motion arrives

Always purposeful. Motion signals direction — where something came from, where it's going. A toast slides in from the top-right because that's where the bell icon lives.

Reduced motion

All animations honor prefers-reduced-motion. Springs collapse to fades, pulses stay static. Function never depends on motion.

Hover lift — buttons
translateY(-1px) + brightness(1.06) · 160ms

All solid buttons lift on hover and settle on press. Shadow deepens with the lift.

Row highlight — tables
background #f6faf6 · 120ms

Glass surfaces
Dark glass — nav on brand Light glass — app top bar

Two sanctioned glass recipes: dark (rgba(255,255,255,0.08) + blur 12–18px, on forest green) and light (rgba(255,255,255,0.82) + blur 14px, for sticky app chrome). Never stack glass on glass.
